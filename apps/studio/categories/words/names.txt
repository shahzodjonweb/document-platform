# Names no common word stands for (facts.common): given names, surnames that have no -ov or -ev shape, places and
# brands a paper may name, lower case, one per line, read folded (facts.fold), so one spelling serves the three
# languages ("malika" is Малика and Malika, "tashkent" is Ташкент). A capitalised word that is one of them, or one with
# a case ending ("Malikaga", "Малике", "Smith's", "Toshkentlik"), is never read as a common word at a sentence's,
# a cell's or an item's start, even where it is also one ("Umid", "Grace", "Надежда", "Qarshi", "Brown"): the owner's
# tie-break, a blank before a leak. Words used as brands that are a language's everyday words ("Yangi", "Xalq",
# "Oila", "Prime") are left out: a paper opens with them every day, and such a name is held where its kind, legal form
# or quotation marks follow it.
# Each section is read in its language, named in its header (facts.NAME_SECTIONS): in a paper of another language a
# name of it refuses only its own language's words, so an English given name ("joy", "tom", "sara") is an English
# paper's name and never refuses an Uzbek word ("Joy ijarasi", "Tom yopish") in an Uzbek paper. The Uzbek names, the
# places and the brands refuse in every paper. A name that is a word of its own paper's language ("Asal", "Baker",
# "Black") is still held there: an accepted false blank, named in tests/test_category_grounding_matrix.py.
#
